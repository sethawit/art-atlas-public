3. **Cézanne "Standing Bather, Seen from the Back" ↔ "Bathers"** (both AIC) — investigated further in a resumption pass and **resolved: not publishable, closed rather than left open**. AIC's own scholarly catalogue traces a recurring bather figure/pose through a documented sequence (FWN 947–55, 966, 973, 2042, 2246-TA, 3003-45a, 3007-46b, 3018-x2a) said to include AIC's "Bathers." To pin down exactly which of our two same-named AIC paintings that is, fetched both live from the AIC API: "Bathers" (slug `bathers`, id 109418) = accession **1973.672**, 1890–94, 22.6×35.4cm; "The Bathers" (slug `the-bathers`, id 45240) = accession **1942.457**, 1899–1904. The dimensions/date match confirms accession 1973.672 = FWN 946 = our "Bathers." But that same catalogue entry flags FWN 946 itself as **"likely... a copy by another hand"** of a *different* Cézanne composition (FWN 955, not in our corpus) — meaning the chain from "Standing Bather" to our "Bathers" runs through a work whose own status as an autograph Cézanne is already in question. Publishing a relationship on that foundation would be citing a citation that's already shaky, not a clean documented fact about these two specific objects. Left unpublished. Source: https://www.artic.edu/digital-publications/47/cezanne-paintings-and-watercolors-at-the-art-institute-of-chicago/344/cat-3-standing-bather-seen-from-the-back-1879-82

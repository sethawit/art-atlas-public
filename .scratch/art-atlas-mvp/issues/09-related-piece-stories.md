To pin down exactly which of our two same-named AIC paintings that is, fetched both live from the AIC API: "Bathers" (slug `bathers`, id 109418) = accession **1973.672**, 1890–94, 22.6×35.4cm; "The Bathers" (slug `the-bathers`, id 45240) = accession **1942.457**, 1899–1904. Publishing a relationship on that foundation would be citing a citation that's already shaky, not a clean documented fact about these two specific objects. Left unpublished.
